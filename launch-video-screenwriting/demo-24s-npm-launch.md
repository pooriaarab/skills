# Worked demo: a 24 second npm-package launch video

A full screenplay for a fictional package, `ogshot`. It reads a README
and renders a cover image for social posts. Every number below is a
stand-in. A real script replaces each one with a measured run.

Logline: a bare link card gets its cover image from one command.

## Beats

| Beat | Seconds | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hook | 0 to 3 | State the pain flatly |
| 2. Cost | 3 to 7 | Show the grey card skipped in a feed |
| 3. Turn | 7 to 12 | Type the command, watch the render |
| 4. Proof | 12 to 19 | The finished card in the same feed, with the time |
| 5. Afterthought | 19 to 24 | End card: command plus repository URL |

Frame 0 shows the grey card. The end card shows the same card finished.
The opening and closing images mirror each other.

The recurring prop is the cursor. It blinks on an empty terminal line
in beat 3 and rests on the finished render in beat 5.

## Second-by-second breakdown

| Time | Visual | Sound | On-screen text | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1 | Grey link card on white | Music starts, low | Your README has no cover image. | Hook in plain words, no greeting |
| 1 to 3 | Card stays grey, cursor hovers, moves on | Music holds | (none) | The hover that leaves is the cost, shown not told |
| 3 to 5 | Feed scrolls: three rich cards pass | Scroll ticks | (none) | Contrast: every post has a card but yours |
| 5 to 7 | Your grey card appears in the feed, scroll passes it | Music dips | Passed over. | Punch word last; value turns from unseen to missed |
| 7 to 9 | Terminal, empty line, cursor blinks | Keystrokes start | (none) | Late in: no setup, straight to the fix |
| 9 to 11 | `npx ogshot` typed and run | Keystrokes, then a soft render hum | npx ogshot | The product enters; the scene flips |
| 11 to 12 | Progress bar fills in one beat | Hum resolves to a click | (none) | Speed shown, not claimed |
| 12 to 15 | Finished card fades in over the grey one | Music lifts | (none) | Same frame as 0 to 1, now fixed |
| 15 to 17 | Card drops into the same feed from beat 2 | Music holds | (none) | The return that changes the meaning |
| 17 to 19 | Cursor stops on the card; time stamp fades in | One soft tick | 4.1 s. One command. | Count as proof; stand-in number |
| 19 to 24 | End card: command, URL, small card thumbnail | Music resolves, then out | npx ogshot / github.com/example/ogshot | The ask as fact; 5 seconds to read and type |

Voiceover, all of it: "Your README has no cover image. One command
renders one. Four seconds." The voice states what happens. The footage
states why it matters. Neither repeats the other.

No plea closes the video. No tagline follows the card. The video ends
at 24 seconds on the held card.
