# Devices

- Lightning fits iPhone 5 through iPhone 14, and the 2016, 2020, and 2022 SE.
- USB-C fits iPhone 15 and later.
- iPhone 5 and 5c stop at iOS 10.
- iPhone 5s and 6 stop at iOS 12.
- Home-button phones do not use the bottom-edge home swipe; use the AssistiveTouch Home item.
- go_back is an edge swipe and is wrong when a back chevron is on screen.

## Proof

- Prove the round pointer before any recipe.
- Do not claim an unproven phone works.
