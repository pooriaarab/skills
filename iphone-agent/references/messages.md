# Messages

Open the app. Read the title. Leave. Do not open a thread. Do not type.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Stay on the home screen. The dock is visible.
2. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
3. Click only when the grey circle sits on the green speech-bubble icon.
4. On this frame that icon center was about (0.38, 0.94).
5. A click with the circle on that icon opened Messages.
6. The title bar read Messages. Edit was on the left. The list was the first screen.
7. Stop there.

A circle on the Search button above the dock is not the icon. Do not click it.

## Leave

1. Move the circle onto the home indicator at the bottom edge.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -600`, then `release`.
3. The frame showed the app switcher. Messages was one card. Calendar was another card.
4. Do not drag a card up. That closes the app.
5. Move the circle into the dark area above the cards.
6. Click. The frame showed the home screen. The dock was visible.

`POST /api/home` is a slow drag. On this phone it opens the switcher. Do not trust it until the frame shows the home screen.
