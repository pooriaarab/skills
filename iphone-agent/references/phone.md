# Phone

Open the app. Read the title. Leave. Do not call. Do not tap a contact.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Stay on the home screen. The dock is visible.
2. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
3. Click only when the grey circle sits on the green handset icon.
4. On this frame that icon center was about (0.15, 0.93).
5. A click there opened Phone.
6. The title read Calls. The bottom bar showed Calls, Contacts, and Keypad.
7. Stop there.

## Leave

1. Move the circle onto the home indicator.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -600`, then `release`.
3. The frame showed the app switcher. One card was labeled Phone.
4. Do not drag a card up.
5. Raw `home`, then a short move, put the circle in the dark area above the cards.
6. Click. The frame showed the home screen.
