# Facebook

Open the app. Read the chrome. Leave. Do not post, like, comment, or open the composer.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Open the Social folder. See [Recipes](recipes.md). Click only when the grey circle is on the folder icon.
2. The folder page showed Facebook at the top left.
3. Move the circle onto the blue icon. Keep it off the badge.
4. Click.
5. The header showed the word facebook, a menu, plus, search, and a messenger mark.
6. The bottom bar showed home, video, a shop, a heart, a bell, and a profile.
7. Stop there. Do not tap the bar. Do not tap plus.

## Leave

1. A drag that starts on the feed scrolls the feed. Do not do that.
2. Move the circle into the margin below the tab bar. Keep it off the icons.
3. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -900`, then `release`.
4. The frame showed the app switcher. One card was labeled Facebook.
5. Do not drag a card up.
6. Raw `home`, then a short move, put the circle in the dark area above the cards.
7. The first click did nothing. A second click showed the home screen.
