# Notifications

Open Notification Centre. Read the title. Leave. Do not open a card.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Stay on the home screen.
2. Raw `home` parks the pointer at the top-left. The circle can sit off the frame.
3. Raw `move 45 18` put the circle on the left status bar, left of the time, above the weather widget.
4. Look. The circle must not sit on a widget.
5. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 520`, then `release`.
6. The frame showed Notification Centre. The title read Notification Centre.
7. Stop there. Do not tap a card. Do not tap the close mark. Do not tap flashlight, Sleep, or camera.

A drag that starts on the right status icons opens Control Centre. That is the wrong panel.

## Close Control Centre

1. Do not tap power, plus, VPN, a radio, flashlight, camera, or playback.
2. Move the circle into empty dark wallpaper.
3. Click. The frame showed the home screen.

## Leave Notification Centre

1. Move the circle onto the home indicator. Keep it off Sleep.
2. From the title area, raw `move 0 180` put the circle on the indicator once. Look before you trust that move.
3. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -700`, then `release`.
4. Wait. Read the frame. The home screen came back. A mid frame looked dim.
