# Instagram

Open the app. Read the chrome. Leave. Do not like, comment, message, or open a story.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Open the Social folder. See [Recipes](recipes.md).
2. The Instagram icon is at the top right of page 1, beside Messenger.
3. A circle on Messenger is not on Instagram. Move right until the circle sits on the camera glyph.
4. Keep the circle off the badge.
5. Click.
6. The title read Instagram. The header showed plus and a heart.
7. The bottom bar showed home, video, a paper plane, search, and a profile.
8. Stop there. Do not tap the heart. Do not open a story.

## Leave

1. Move the circle onto the home indicator, below the tab bar.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -900`, then `release`.
3. The frame showed the app switcher. The cards were labeled Facebook and Instagram.
4. Do not drag a card up.
5. Raw `home`, then `move 36 24`, put the circle in the dark area above the labels.
6. The first click did nothing. A second click showed the home screen.
