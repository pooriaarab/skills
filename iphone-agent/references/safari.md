# Safari

Open the app. Read the bottom bar. Leave. Do not type. Do not follow a link.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Stay on the home screen. The dock is visible.
2. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
3. Click only when the grey circle sits on the blue compass icon.
4. On this frame that icon center was about (0.62, 0.93).
5. A circle just above the icon is not on it. Move down, then look again.
6. A click with the circle on the compass opened Safari.
7. The bottom bar showed back, forward, an address, reload, and tabs.
8. Stop there. Do not copy the page into the recipe.

## Leave

1. Move the circle onto the home indicator under the bottom bar.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -600`, then `release`.
3. The frame showed the app switcher. One card was labeled Safari.
4. Do not drag a card up.
5. Raw `home`, then a short move, put the circle in the dark area above the cards.
6. Click. The frame showed the home screen. The dock was visible.
