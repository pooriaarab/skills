# Settings

Open the app. Read the title. Leave. Do not change a switch.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Where it sits

A horizontal drag on empty wallpaper, above Search, moved to the next Home Screen page. That page showed a gear labeled Settings. Mail was on the same page. Camera was not on that page. See [Camera](camera.md).

## Open

1. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
2. Click only when the grey circle sits on the gear, not on the label.
3. The app opened on the AssistiveTouch page.
4. The back chevron went to a page titled Touch, then to a page titled Accessibility.
5. No switch was changed. Stop there.

## Leave

The home indicator can sit over the last row. Do not click that row. Raw `press`, `move 0 -700`, and `release` opened the app switcher. A card was labeled Settings. A click in the dark area showed the home screen.
