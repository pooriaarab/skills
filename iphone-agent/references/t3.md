# Dock folder

The dark dock icon is a folder. It is not one app.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open the folder

1. Stay on the home screen.
2. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
3. Click only when the grey circle sits on the dark dock icon.
4. On this frame that icon was to the right of Safari, near (0.81, 0.93).
5. The folder title is Productivity. It showed two icons. The labels were T3 Code and Muse.
6. Do not call the folder Muse or T3.

## T3 Code

1. Click only when the grey circle sits on the T3 icon inside the folder.
2. The first screen was a developer sheet. The name line read T3 Code. A Continue button was at the bottom.
3. Do not tap Continue.
4. The app then showed the title T3 Code ALPHA.
5. Do not open search, the gear, a project, or a pull request.
6. Leave from the home indicator. A short drag does not leave this app. The circle must sit on the home indicator. Then raw `press`, `move 0 -800`, and `release` opened the app switcher.
7. A click in the dark area above the cards showed the home screen.

## Muse

This open is from 2026-10-16. The frame was 500 by 1080.

1. Click only when the grey circle sits on the Muse icon. The icon is white with a blue M.
2. The first frame showed a blue M and the word Meta.
3. The next frame showed the app. The name was Muse. A field was labeled Message. The bottom bar had five icons.
4. Do not open a thread. Do not send a message.
5. Leave from the home indicator. Raw `press`, `move 0 -800`, and `release` opened the app switcher.
6. A click in the dark area above the cards showed the home screen.
