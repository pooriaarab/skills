# Camera

Open the system Camera app from App Library. Leave. Do not press the shutter.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-16. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Find it

1. The system Camera icon was not on the first three Home Screen pages.
2. Do not open a folder whose name contains the word Camera.
3. A page drag must start on empty wallpaper. Do not start on the status bar, an icon, or the left edge.
4. From the folder page, one more left drag opened App Library.
5. The search field label read App Library.

## Open

1. Click the search field. A cursor and Cancel appeared.
2. Send raw `type Camera`. Do not type any other word.
3. The first row was Camera. Its icon was a lens.
4. Later rows were other apps. Do not click them.
5. Click only when the grey circle sits on the first Camera row.
6. The first frame can still show the list. Wait, then save again.
7. The Camera app opened. PHOTO was selected.
8. The chrome showed a grid, flash off, live off, a night icon, and zoom steps 0.5, 1x, and 2.5.
9. The mode row showed VIDEO, PHOTO, and PORTRAIT. A white shutter and a flip control were on screen.
10. Do not press the shutter. Do not change the mode.

## Leave

1. Move the grey circle onto the home indicator.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -900`, then `release`.
3. The first frame can still show PHOTO. Wait, then save again.
4. A later frame showed the app switcher. The cards were TikTok and Camera. App Library was behind them.
5. Do not drag a card up. Do not click a card title.
6. A click in the dark area above the cards returned to App Library. That is a leave.
