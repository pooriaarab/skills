# LinkedIn

Open the app. Read the chrome. Leave. Do not open a post, a profile, or a message
— unless running the warmup scripts (see [Warmup](warmup.md)).

This page records one phone on 2026-10-16. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Open the Social folder. See [Recipes](recipes.md).
2. LinkedIn is the blue icon at the left of the second row.
3. Move the grey circle onto the upper right of that icon. Keep it off a badge.
4. Click.
5. The screen showed a Search field.
6. The bottom bar showed Home, Network, Notifications, Video, and Jobs.
7. The feed was still placeholder bars. Stop there. Do not tap the bar.

## Warmup script

```bash
warmup-linkedin [--rounds N] [--auto]
```

Does: feed scroll, like, comment, connection requests, notifications, network. Randomized.

## Leave

1. A wide grey area on the tab bar is not the pointer. Wiggle, then read the frame.
2. The home indicator sat at the very bottom, under the tab icons.
3. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -900`, then `release`.
4. The frame showed the app switcher. One card was titled Chat. One card was LinkedIn.
5. Do not drag a card up. Do not click a card title.
6. A click in the dark area above the labels returned to the Social folder.
7. The folder was still open. That is a leave.
