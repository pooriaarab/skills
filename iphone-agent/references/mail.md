# Mail

Open the app. Read the header. Leave. Do not tap compose. Do not open a message.

This page records one phone on 2026-10-15. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Use the next Home Screen page. See [Settings](settings.md) for that drag.
2. The blue envelope is labeled Mail. On this frame it sat at the lower right of the icon grid.
3. Move the pointer. Save the frame.
4. Click only when the grey circle sits on the envelope.
5. Mail opened. The header was blue. A mailbox title was visible. A plus button was visible.
6. Stop there. Do not tap the plus button.

## Leave

The next frame showed the home screen. Do not send mail.
