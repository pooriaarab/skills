# X

Open the app. Read the chrome. Leave. Do not open a thread. Do not send
— unless running the warmup scripts (see [Warmup](warmup.md)).

This page records one phone on 2026-10-16. The frame was 500 by 1080.

## Open

1. Open the Social folder. See [Recipes](recipes.md).
2. X is the dark icon in the center of the bottom row of page 1.
3. Click only when the grey circle sits on that icon.
4. The title read Chat.
5. A Search field was on the screen.
6. The bottom bar showed home, search, a swirl, a bell, and a chat icon.
7. Stop there. Do not open a thread.

## Warmup script

```bash
warmup-x [--rounds N] [--auto]
```

Does: timeline scroll, like, reply, retweet, DMs, notifications. Randomized.

## Leave

1. Move the grey circle onto the home indicator, below the bar.
2. Send raw `press`, then `move 0 -900`, then `release`.
3. The frame showed the app switcher. Cards included Muse and X.
4. Do not drag a card up. Do not click a card title.
5. A click in the dark area above the cards returned to the Social folder.
6. The folder was still open. That is a leave.
