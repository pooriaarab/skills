# Pooria Photo

Use this skill only when Pooria Arab is the subject.

## Load the references

- Always read [references/identity.md](references/identity.md) before generation or review.
- Read [references/realistic-capture.md](references/realistic-capture.md) when writing or revising a prompt.

## Select source photos

Use three to five original photos from different angles.

Include one close face, one three-quarter view, and one body reference.

Use originals as identity references. Never use generated images to establish identity.

Use a generated image only for composition when the user requests that exact composition.

Inspect every selected source before generation.

## Build one believable moment

Give each image one job and one scene.

State Pooria's action, attention, and the moment immediately before the frame.

Choose one capture frame. Good defaults include a handheld phone, compact camera, or documentary event photo.

Keep props few, ordinary, and visibly used.

Use available light, normal background clutter, natural skin, and slightly imperfect framing.

Ask for lightly haired hands and forearms. Match the source photos without adding dense body hair.

Avoid staged poses, studio backdrops, direct eye contact, and centered symmetry unless the image needs them.

Avoid polish words such as `8K`, `masterpiece`, `cinematic`, `luxury`, and `flawless`.

## Generate and compare

Generate one candidate at a time.

Compare it beside the original references at full size.

Reject the image when any core trait fails:

- Glasses are not round, thin, and dark.
- Face or jaw width changes.
- Hairline, hair shape, or eyebrow weight changes.
- Beard density, outline, or moustache connection changes.
- Body proportions drift from Pooria's lean frame.
- Hands deform or body hair becomes excessive.

Then review the photograph itself:

- The pose should look interrupted, not held.
- The gaze should follow the scene.
- Clothing should wrinkle and hang naturally.
- Light should come from a plausible source.
- Background objects should support the moment.
- The crop should remain useful on desktop and mobile.

Revise only the weakest dimension before the next generation.

Stop when identity and realism both pass. Do not accept a polished look with a wrong face.

## Delivery

Keep the original generated file.

Create a compressed website copy when needed.

Report the output path, source references, prompt, crop, and intended section.
