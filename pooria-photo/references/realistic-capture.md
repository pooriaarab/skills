# Realistic capture patterns

Use one capture pattern per image. Mixing several creates a polished composite.

## Useful capture patterns

### Handheld phone

- Eye-level or slightly low angle.
- Minor tilt or imperfect crop.
- Uneven natural light.
- Slight motion blur in a hand or background person.
- Ordinary color and no beauty retouching.

### Friend across the table

- Pooria looks toward the person, not the lens.
- A cup or shoulder can partly block the frame.
- The table shows used objects and small clutter.
- The expression happens mid-conversation.

### Small event

- Mixed window and room light.
- People enter the frame edges.
- Pooria gestures mid-sentence.
- Chairs, cables, and tables remain visible.
- Avoid stages, microphones, and brand walls.

### Outdoor walk

- Capture mid-step with clothing in motion.
- Use soft overcast light or ordinary late-day light.
- Let wind change the hair slightly.
- Keep the setting plausible and understated.

### Working session

- Pooria focuses on a screen or collaborator.
- Include a used notebook, cable, and water glass.
- Keep the desk imperfect.
- Show one clear action instead of generic typing.

### Quiet personal moment

- Browse a book, make coffee, cook, wait for transit, or pack a bag.
- Keep the expression neutral or lightly amused.
- Use normal clothes from the references.
- Avoid symbolic props and inspirational staging.

## Prompt order

Write prompts in this order:

1. Identity contract and original reference rule.
2. Current action and attention.
3. Place and ordinary supporting details.
4. Capture frame and light source.
5. Crop and responsive space.
6. Identity failures to prevent.
7. Scene-specific exclusions.

## Research basis

These repositories informed the patterns above. Adapt their ideas instead of copying full prompts.

- [airoasting/gpt-image](https://github.com/airoasting/gpt-image/blob/main/references/prompt-craft.md) recommends one capture context and ordinary imperfections.
- [cloverforks/ai-gpt-image-2-prompts](https://github.com/cloverforks/ai-gpt-image-2-prompts/blob/main/categories/photography-realism.md) includes identity-preserving edits and imperfect phone snapshots.
- [Toolcentral-ai/awesome-gpt-image-2-prompts](https://github.com/Toolcentral-ai/awesome-gpt-image-2-prompts) provides a source-attributed prompt catalog and metadata.
- [EddieTYP/image-prompt-library](https://github.com/EddieTYP/image-prompt-library) keeps prompts, results, references, and provenance together.

Preserve source links and license details when reusing any prompt text.
