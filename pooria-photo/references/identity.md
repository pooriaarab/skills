# Pooria identity reference

Treat original photos as the authority. These notes help detect drift.

## Stable traits

- Face: oval, with a moderate jaw. Avoid a narrow V-shaped chin.
- Skin: medium warm-olive tone with natural variation.
- Hair: thick, dark, and brushed upward. Keep the real hairline and slight asymmetry.
- Eyebrows: thick, dark, and gently arched.
- Eyes: brown, with a relaxed expression.
- Glasses: thin, dark, round wire frames. This trait is mandatory.
- Nose: preserve its real width and profile.
- Beard: short, full, and naturally uneven. Keep the connected moustache.
- Build: lean, with moderate shoulders and long limbs.
- Hands: slim fingers with light or moderate hair. Do not exaggerate forearm hair.

## Common generation failures

- Rectangular acetate glasses replace the round wire frames.
- The jaw becomes too narrow or too square.
- The beard becomes a sharp fashion outline.
- Hair becomes a high, sculpted pompadour.
- Skin becomes smooth, bright, or heavily retouched.
- The torso becomes broad or muscular.
- Hands and forearms gain dense dark hair.
- Every pose uses a notebook, laptop, or direct camera gaze.

Reject these results. Do not hide identity drift with a smaller crop.
