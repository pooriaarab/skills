# Style: Patch bay

First used for: foxplug (27.0 s), a browser tool that plugs an agent straight into web apps.

The whole video is a modular-synth patch panel: matte black, metal jacks with small lamps, and thick
patch cables that draw on.

## Use it for
Products that make a direct connection with nothing in between: connectors, integrations, keys pinned to
one host. A patch cable is the plainest picture of a direct link, and a jack that takes only one cable shows
a key bound to one place.

## Look
- **Ground:** one solid matte black `#16171b`. Nothing moves behind the graphics.
- **Palette:** cream text `#f2ead8`, dim `#9a958a`, brand orange `#ff7a1a` (the main cable, the name,
  Approve), gold `#ffc23d` (keys), teal `#34d1bf` (a third cable), lamp green `#3ddc84`, lamp red `#ff5a4f`,
  steel `#3a3d45` (a server box), card `#2a2c33`.
- **Type pair:** Barlow Condensed 700/600 for display (a panel-label feel) and Barlow 600 for labels;
  JetBrains Mono 500 for the install line, the URL and host tags.
- **The agent:** a glowing orb in the brand color with a cream sparkle. Never a robot.

## Signature graphics
- Patch cables (cubic sag, 16 px, drop shadow) that draw on, with light dots running along them.
- Metal jacks (radial steel ring, black hole) with a lamp that turns green or red.
- Brand marks (Simple Icons, CC0, in their own colors) on white chips.
- A steel "server" box that falls off the panel; synth knobs that turn on one after another.

## Copy pattern
PAS. Problem: a server sits in between. Agitate into solve: the box drops, "no server". Then the name and
the promise ("Your keys never leave the browser."). End card: the install command and the repository URL.

## Storyboard and pacing
- 9 shots in 27 s. Sentence shots (1, 4 and 9) hold 3.75 s or more. Graphic shots with a 2 to 5 word label
  hold 2.1 to 3.2 s.
- Seams: hard cuts on the beat grid (112 BPM, beat 0.5357 s, phase 0.032 s).

## Text entrances
None repeats back to back: finished at frame 0 (the poster), mask wipe, stamp, blur-to-sharp name slam with
a slide-in line, rise, stepped type wipe, wipe, rise, per-letter rise on the end card.

## Music
`elevenlabs/music`, 27 s, `force_instrumental`:

> Modular analog synth groove instrumental, 112 BPM, bubbling sequenced synth arpeggio, warm round bass,
> punchy tight drum machine with crisp claps, bright and curious, confident tech-launch energy. Drums and
> arpeggio from the very first second, no intro. A lift with fuller drums at 12 seconds. Clean ending on the
> final bar at 27 seconds. No vocals.

Chosen over `minimax/music-02` (same prompt) because it lands on a clean final bar at 27 s; the minimax take
ran to its end at full level.

## SFX cue map
Whoosh as the key slides into the server; pop and stamp on "no server"; hit on the name; click when the key
plugs in, glitch when it bounces off; soft pops on the brand chips; clicks on the knobs; click on Approve,
chime on the green lamp; sparkle on the end card. Volumes 0.2 to 0.45.
