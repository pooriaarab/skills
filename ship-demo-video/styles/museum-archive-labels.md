# Museum archive labels

First used on foxlens. The product is the collection: each harness still hangs on a gallery wall as a framed exhibit, with a tombstone wall label.

- **Ground:** a warm gallery wall `#ebe4d6` under the scene hosts. It has two radial gradients: a warm spotlight pool at the upper left and a soft dark falloff at the edges. There is a baseboard at y 1040. The spotlight comes up from 0.6 to 1 opacity in the first 2.4 s. That is the only drift.
- **Type pair:** Libre Caslon Text (Google woff2, own `@font-face`, roman, bold and italic) for the statements, the wall-label titles and the bodies. Use italic for the turn of a line ("are only paint.", "never left"). Source Code Pro (bundled) for the accession lines and the metadata, in muted `#5e554a` with 0.06em tracking. Uppercase it for the kicker lines.
- **Exhibits:** each still sits in a 22 px dark frame (`#201b16`) on a white mat, with a deep soft shadow. The image area is 1040 x 650 at x 130. Each exhibit has two shots. Shot A shows the whole page with a "Exhibit I" label. Shot B pushes in on the detail (the drawn button, the outline, the page log) with a second label ("The request", "The point", "The page replies").
- **Wall label:** a 580 px off-white card with a 1 px border. It has a kicker ("EXHIBIT II" in 20 px mono caps), a 50 px italic title, a 31 px body, and an accession line in 23 px mono. Every value on a label comes from the run's `result.json` or the E2E check list.
- **Text entrances:**
  - Masked per-line rise for the statements (`yPercent` 110 to 0, 0.6 s, `power3.out`, 0.12 s apart).
  - Label "placed on the wall": a 24 px rise while its shadow grows.
  - The accession line types out in frame steps (0.012 s per character). Keep it fast enough to finish inside a 1.2 s shot.
  - The kicker under a wordmark slides 60 px with a fade. Do not tween `letterSpacing`: lint rejects it (`gsap_non_transform_motion`).
- **Seams:** two kinds. A dolly pan along the wall: the next frame and image enter with x 520 to 0 (0.55 s), as if the camera moves to the next exhibit. A lights dip: a dark overlay goes to 0.9 in 0.11 s and back up in 0.3 s, on the act seams (wordmark, exhibit I, the privacy statement, exhibit III, proof, end).
- **Pacing:** shots of 4 ticks (1.2 s at 100 BPM). The privacy statement holds for 8 ticks (2.4 s), because it carries the message. 15 shots in 21.5 s. No avatar.
- **Music prompt:** "Elegant curious neo-classical chamber piece for a museum, 100 BPM. Pizzicato strings, felt piano, soft woodblock and brushed percussion. Starts on a clear downbeat with no long intro, the full string section and a pulse enter at about 7 seconds, warm and confident, clean ending on the final bar. Instrumental, no vocals." The minimax/music-2.6 take, trimmed from 4.5 s, won: it has a near-silent breath just before 7.2 s, then the full entry lands on Exhibit II. It came in quiet, so `loudnorm=I=-13` it before the build (the final mix measures -16 LUFS).
- **SFX cue map:** soft pop on the hook swap. Stamp (0.3) on the wordmark and on the proof number. Whoosh on each dolly pan. Type under the accession lines. Chime on "Found". A low hit on the privacy statement. Click on clickAt. Chime on the end card. Keep every effect at 0.2 to 0.45. It is a gallery, not an arcade.
- **Copy pattern:** a hook in two beats ("Some buttons / are only paint."), then the stakes ("Your agent still has to click them."). Then the wordmark with a mono kicker. Then numbered exhibits, each with a title in curator voice ("Subscribe, painted", "Found, and outlined", "clickAt, delivered") and a plain body sentence. The privacy claim is a three-line statement over its raw field values. "Also in the collection" lists the other measured cases. Then the proof count and the end card.
