# Journals, magazines and editorial outlets (extended)

Date: 2026-10-16. Research only. Nothing was submitted, registered, posted or logged in.
Not repeated here (already in `paper-submission/references/venues.md` and `free-venues.md`): TMLR, DMLR, JMLR MLOSS, JOSS, JAIR, TACL, ReScience C, Journal of Open Research Software.
Author profile assumed: independent author, no affiliation, work already on arXiv.

## How to read this file

- UNVERIFIED means the official page did not load or did not say. No cell was filled from memory.
- "(search snippet)" means the fact came from a search result of the official domain. The page itself was blocked (HTTP 403). Confirm before you rely on it.
- Elsevier, Cell Press, ACM, MIT Press, Wiley and Springer pages block plain fetches. the second part read ACM and Springer with headless Chrome. the first part and the third part could not read Elsevier, Cell or ACM magazine pages.
- Two Elsevier pages for one journal sometimes give two APC numbers. Both are shown. Confirm at submission.
- Indexing is what the official page says. "Not stated on official page" means the page loaded and has no list.

Findings that change the plan:

1. No free and fast open-access journal fits "AI benchmarks" better than DMLR and TMLR (already in the other file). In this list, Computational Linguistics and Computo are the free peer-reviewed options.
2. Every ACM journal now charges. The old "free to publish in ACM" idea is wrong for 2026.
3. The New Stack closed its guest program (update 2026-07-17). Distill is on hiatus. Pragmatic Engineer guest posts and Increment are closed. See Table 2.
4. Frontiers, Scientific Data, Patterns, SoftwareX and Data in Brief have APCs of USD 1,300 to 4,900. No independent-author waiver is stated for any of them.
5. F1000Research says an author needs an accredited institution, and also says an author without one "should state your independent status". The page contradicts itself. Ask the office first.

## Cross-cutting traps

- Double-anonymous journals (AI and Ethics, ACM JRC, ACM TAISAP) conflict with a public arXiv paper. Read each page on this point.
- IEEE magazines say "We don't accept papers that have been published elsewhere". An arXiv copy is not named. Disclose it and rewrite for practitioners.
- Several publishers want an institutional email (Computational Linguistics, AI and Ethics). Prepare ORCID, a publication record and a short note about independent status.
- Elsevier hybrid journals: the open-access APC is optional. The subscription route has no author fee (search snippet for AIJ). JSS and IST pages did not load, so confirm.
- Frontiers: the affiliation field must show where the work was done. A false "unaffiliated" institution can be an ethics breach (page text). Use the real independent status.
