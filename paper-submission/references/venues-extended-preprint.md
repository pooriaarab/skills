# Free preprint servers, archives and indexing services (extended)

Checked 2026-10-16. Task: find free preprint servers, archives, repositories and indexing services beyond `free-venues.md` and `venues.md` in `pooriaarab/skills/paper-submission/references`.

Rules for reading the tables:

- "Not stated" means the official page loaded and said nothing on that point.
- `UNVERIFIED` means the official page did not load (403, 404, 522, bot wall, empty page) or no official page was found.
- Search-result snippets are not official pages. Where a fact comes only from a snippet, the cell says so.
- Nothing was submitted, registered or logged in. Re-open the source URL before you act.
- "GS" means Google Scholar. No page below states that the venue itself is indexed by GS. Google's own inclusion page lists only personal sites, repositories and journal hosts. It names no preprint server.

Skipped because the two reference files already cover them: arXiv, Research Square, SSRN, Cambridge Open Engage, Preprints.org, Wiley Research Exchange/Authorea, Zenodo, Figshare, Hugging Face dataset repos, Software Heritage, SciELO Preprints, engrXiv, Open Research Europe, HAL, Qeios, Internet Archive Scholar, CORE, Papers with Code, OSF Preprints, TechRxiv, TMLR, DMLR, JMLR MLOSS, JOSS, JAIR, TACL, ReScience C.

## 0. What matters most

1. Only two new venues are true fits for a CS paper by an independent author with a personal email: ScienceOpen (free, ORCID needed) and OpenReview (as an account, for venue submissions). Both have traps (see rows).
2. IACR ePrint and ECCC are free, open to any author, and well regarded. They take only cryptology and computational complexity.
3. Jxiv (Japan) and IndiaRxiv bar a paper that is already posted elsewhere. That conflicts with an arXiv copy. IndiaRxiv is also paused.
4. AfricArXiv needs an Africa link. CEUR-WS and EPTCS take volumes from organisers, not single papers.
5. viXra, ai.viXra and rxiVerse are open to anyone. They are not reputable. See the avoid table. Do not use them for a paper you want taken seriously.
6. Hugging Face Papers and alphaXiv sit on top of arXiv. They help discovery. They do not host a paper that is not on arXiv.
7. Google Scholar accepts a paper on your own website. Google's page says so in plain words.

## G. Gaps and next steps

- ResearchGate, Academia.edu, aiXiv, ai-archive.io, SSOAR, dblp, BASE, Harvard Dataverse and ACM did not load. A person must open them.
- Cost, DOI and GS cells for Jxiv, ChinaXiv and Sciencepaper Online need a Chinese or Japanese reader and a logged-in view.
- No page in this set says that the venue itself is indexed by Google Scholar.
- No new venue beats arXiv plus a personal site for reach. For a CS or AI paper, ScienceOpen is the only new general host that is free, open to an independent author and gives a Crossref DOI.
