# Layout catalogue

Put the layout name on each slide as `data-layout="<name>"`. The pre-flight
fails when two slides in a row share one. Pick by what the slide must do.

| Layout | Use it for | Build | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `statement` | The hook. One bold line, full bleed. | Headline 120–160 px, left or centred, lots of empty space. | More than 12 words. Split it. |
| `giant-number` | One number that lands the point. | Number 280–400 px in the accent colour, one 44 px line beside it. | A number with no source id. |
| `split` | A picture and a short line. | Image bleeds off one half; text on the other, aligned to the image's centre line. | Text that floats on a busy photo. |
| `anatomy` | How a thing works. | One anatomy figure fills 70% of the stage; a 96 px caption names the idea. | A figure built for another deck. |
| `quote-card` | Social proof. | A card with the verbatim quote at 56–72 px, then name, handle and date at 32–36 px. | A paraphrase in quote marks. |
| `before-after` | Change. | Two panes, the "before" muted, the "after" in full colour, one word over each. | Two equal panes with no winner. |
| `diagram` | A flow or a loop of 3–5 steps. | Large shapes and arrows, one label each, no legend. | A diagram that needs a sentence to read. |
| `one-word` | A beat or a pause (Takahashi). | One word, 300+ px. | Using it twice in a row. |
| `screenshot` | Proof the product exists. | A real crop, scaled up, one callout ring and a 44 px label. | A full window at 30% scale. |
| `ask` | The close. | The one action, the URL or handle, nothing else. | A "Questions?" slide. |

**Rhythm.** Open with `statement`, close with `ask`. Put a quiet slide after
every dense one. Use at least 6 layouts in 10 slides, and at most two quote cards.

## Light and dark

Design both themes; never invert one into the other. Dark: warm near-black,
off-white text, one saturated accent. Light: warm paper, near-black text, the
accent darkened to pass 4.5:1. Swap figure art per theme (anatomy `--light`).
