# News method

Use this method to build a news block from the last 1 to 4 weeks. Choose the window by
how fast your field moves. Use 1 week for fast fields. Use 4 weeks for slow fields.

## Steps

1. Write the window as a start date and an end date.
2. List 8 to 12 candidates from primary sources: vendor blogs, changelogs, release notes,
   court or regulator filings, papers, and official posts.
3. Add secondary reports only when they link to a primary source. Open the primary source.
4. Open every source. Do not rely on a search snippet or a summary.
5. Keep 3 to 5 items that support your one point. Drop the rest.

## Record for each item

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Claim | One sentence. State only what the source states. |
| URL | The page you opened. |
| Published | The date on the page. Write "not shown" if absent. |
| Accessed | The date you opened it. |
| Quote | Verbatim, in quote marks, with the speaker. Optional. |

## Quote rules

- Copy the text from the page. Do not retype it from memory.
- Keep the original words, spelling and punctuation. Mark a cut with an ellipsis.
- Name the speaker and link the page. If you cannot, do not quote.
- Never put words in quote marks that you paraphrased or translated.
- Never attribute a quote from a screenshot you cannot trace to a page.

## Number rules

- Give a number only when the source states it. Cite the source on the slide notes.
- Keep the unit and the time frame from the source.
- Do not round up, combine or compare numbers from different sources.
- Mark an estimate as an estimate and name who made it.

## Stop conditions

- Stop and drop an item if two sources disagree and you cannot find the primary one.
- Stop and drop an item if the page changed after you read it. Re-read first.
- Hand the verified list to the deck. Keep the source list in the speaker notes.
